__INTRO__

Coverage is the share of segments a factor labels with a real value (not "not applicable", "unspecified", or "none"), averaged over the run's pool. Tree nodes count the root plus every factor that survives redundancy pruning at τ 0.2.

Runs at a glance

Tree nodes count the root plus every combination that survives redundancy pruning at the given threshold τ; a higher τ tolerates more overlap. The first two versions count single factors only; DAG feedback also counts pairs. Click a row to open that run.

Measures (selected version)

All runs scored the same way (), no model calls. Vendi is a diversity score over the pool's partitions with normalized mutual information as similarity: it equals the number of factors when they split the segments independently and 1 when they all split them the same way; it does not depend on τ. Null is the same score after shuffling each factor's labels across segments (20 shuffles): the ceiling for this pool. Junk adds three meaningless but consistent factors (random per video, or per segment) and counts how many survive pruning. Unit coverage is the share of each knowledge unit's segments that get a real value, averaged over units and factors. Granularity is each factor's entropy over only the segments where it applies. Definition spread is a Vendi score over the definition embeddings (cosine similarity).
